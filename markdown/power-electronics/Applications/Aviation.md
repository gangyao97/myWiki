##  Regulatory Standards

- FAA Regulations:

  - 14 CFR Part 21: Certification procedures for products and parts.

  - 14 CFR Part 25: Airworthiness standards for transport category airplanes.

  - 14 CFR Part 33: Airworthiness standards for aircraft engines.

  - 14 CFR Part 23: Airworthiness standards for normal, utility, acrobatic, and commuter category airplanes.

- EASA Regulations:

  - CS-25: Certification specifications for large airplanes.

  - CS-23: Certification specifications for normal category airplanes.

  - CS-E: Certification specifications for engines.

## Industry Standards
- RTCA Standards:

  - [DO-160](https://do160.org/rtca-do-160g/): Environmental testing for airborne equipment (e.g., temperature, vibration, humidity, and EMI/EMC testing).

  - [DO-178](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DO-178C): Software considerations in airborne systems and equipment certification.

  - [DO-254](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DO-254): Design assurance guidance for airborne electronic hardware.
  
  - [DO-311](https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/design_approvals/dah/lithium_batteries): Performance Testing for Rechargeable Lithium Batteries 

- SAE International:

  - [ARP4754](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/arp4754b/): Guidelines for development of civil aircraft and systems.

  - [ARP4761](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/arp4761a/): Guidelines for Conducting the Safety Assessment Process on Civil Aircraft, Systems, and Equipment.
