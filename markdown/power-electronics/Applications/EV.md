##  Functional Safety Standards

Functional safety is critical for EV traction inverters to ensure safe operation under all conditions.

- ISO 26262: Road vehicles – Functional safety.

  - Covers the entire lifecycle of safety-related systems, including hardware and software.

  - Defines Automotive Safety Integrity Levels (ASIL) for risk classification.

- IEC 61508: Functional safety of electrical/electronic/programmable electronic safety-related systems (general industrial standard).
