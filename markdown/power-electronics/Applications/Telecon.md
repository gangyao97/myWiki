##  International Standards

- IEC (International Electrotechnical Commission):

  - IEC 60950-1: Safety of information technology equipment (replaced by IEC 62368-1).

  - IEC 62368-1: Audio/video, information, and communication technology equipment – Safety requirements.

  - IEC 61000: Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) standards for power supplies.

  - IEC 61000-3-2: Limits for harmonic current emissions.

  - IEC 61000-4: Testing and measurement techniques for EMC.

  - IEC 62485: Safety requirements for secondary batteries and battery installations.

- UL (Underwriters Laboratories):
  - UL 60950-1: Safety of information technology equipment (replaced by UL 62368-1).

  - UL 62368-1: Safety requirements for audio/video, information, and communication technology equipment.

  - UL 508: Standard for industrial control equipment, including power supplies.
