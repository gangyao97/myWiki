# Dual Active Bridge (DAB) Converter

A **Dual Active Bridge (DAB) converter** is a bidirectional DC-to-DC power converter that uses two active bridges (one on the primary side and one on the secondary side) to achieve high-efficiency power transfer. It is widely used in applications like electric vehicle charging, renewable energy systems, and energy storage systems.
